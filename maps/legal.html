Primary data and map sources

  • Minnesota Department of Natural Resources: LakeFinder metadata and surveys, fish species references, lake morphology and survey inventories, geographic layers, and other identified records. Minnesota DNR contributed data to this product. Its material is subject to the MNDNR General Data & Software License Agreement.
  • Minnesota Pollution Control Agency: surface-water assessment records.
  • USDA Forest Service: Superior National Forest BWCAW entry-point locations and selected National Forest trail geometry.
  • OpenStreetMap contributors: Street basemap tiles, © OpenStreetMap contributors, under the Open Data Commons Open Database License.
  • U.S. Geological Survey: optional topographic tiles from the USGS National Map.

Planning and safety disclaimer

This website is an experimental planning aid, not a navigational map or a statement of legal access. Routes may contain errors, inferred connections, impassable water, closed portages, outdated conditions, or hazards. Fish surveys describe samples rather than current populations or guarantees. Verify routes, boundaries, permits, closures, weather, water levels, and conditions using current official sources and appropriate navigation materials.
