Coverage & known limitations

Use this site to compare possibilities, not to make safety-critical decisions. The graph is assembled from mapped lakes, streams, portages, and reviewed or approximate connectors. A route appearing on the map does not establish that it is legal, open, navigable, or safe today.

Source snapshotThe current checked-in entry-point source snapshot is from 2026. Individual source datasets have their own dates and refresh procedures in the data provenance record.

Known gaps

  • Some entry approaches are approximate and may attach to the nearest usable graph location rather than a field-verified landing.
  • Some rivers and river-to-lake transitions remain disconnected or incomplete. Mapped perennial water is not proof of canoe navigability.
  • Portage geometry, closures, landings, and trail conditions may differ from the checked-in data.
  • Routes optimize mapped distance. They do not yet account for current, wind, rapids, beaver dams, water level, skill, weather, closures, or travel time.
  • Fisheries results are historical survey samples. Catch rate and survey share do not estimate the current total population or guarantee fishing success.

Before a trip

Confirm the permit and entry rules, current Superior National Forest alerts, fire restrictions, closures, weather, water conditions, and route details from current official sources. Carry appropriate paper or offline navigation and do not depend on this website or cellular service in the field.
