# Third-party data, software, and attribution

The BWCA site combines original project work with data and software from outside
sources. Source-specific terms control the applicable material; the project's
copyright notice does not replace or restrict those terms.

## Data and map services

- **Minnesota Department of Natural Resources (MNDNR):** LakeFinder metadata and
  surveys, species references, basin morphology, surveyed-lake inventory,
  geographic layers, and other identified MNDNR records contribute to this
  product. Use of MNDNR material is subject to the
  [MNDNR General Data & Software License Agreement](https://www.dnr.state.mn.us/sitetools/data_software_license.html).
  MNDNR data is provided as-is, is for reference rather than navigation, and
  does not imply MNDNR endorsement.
- **Minnesota Pollution Control Agency (MPCA):** Surface-water assessment
  records are obtained from the
  [MPCA surface-water assessment service](https://services.pca.state.mn.us/api/doc/v1/surfacewater/assessments).
- **USDA Forest Service:** BWCAW entry-point locations and selected National
  Forest trail geometry come from the Superior National Forest and Forest
  Service Enterprise Data Warehouse sources documented in
  [Data/SOURCES.md](Data/SOURCES.md). Agency data and names do not imply
  endorsement of this project.
- **OpenStreetMap contributors:** The Street basemap is © OpenStreetMap
  contributors and is used under the
  [Open Data Commons Open Database License](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).
- **U.S. Geological Survey:** The optional topographic basemap uses tiles
  provided by the [USGS National Map](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/national-geospatial-program/national-map).

The complete dataset inventory, source URLs, processing notes, and refresh
procedures are maintained in [Data/SOURCES.md](Data/SOURCES.md).
